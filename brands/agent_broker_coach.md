# Agent Broker Coach — Brand Voice Profile

**Who:** Coaching for real estate agents and brokers who want to grow their
business — led by Jody McNamer, a working broker who's built and scaled in the
field. Practical, been-there mentorship, not theory.

**Website / CTA destination:** https://agentbrokercoach.com

## Voice
Motivating, direct, expert, peer-to-peer. Talks to agents like a successful
colleague who's sharing what actually works. Confident and energizing without
hype or "guru" fluff. Real tactics over vague inspiration.

## Audience tracks (rotate across the batch)
- New agents — getting their first deals, building habits.
- Stuck/plateaued agents — breaking through to consistent income.
- Team leaders & brokers — recruiting, systems, scaling.
- Agents drowning in busywork — lead gen, follow-up, time management.

## Content angles to draw from
Lead generation, follow-up scripts, mindset, daily routines, objection handling,
recruiting, social media for agents, market shifts, productivity systems,
common mistakes, quick wins, behind-the-scenes of coaching, client/agent
transformations (kept generic). Tie to Done For You Leads where natural.

## CTAs (vary them)
Book a free coaching call · Join the program · Grab the free training ·
DM "COACH" · Visit agentbrokercoach.com

## HARD RULES (never break)
- No income guarantees or "get rich" claims. No specific earnings promises.
- Don't disparage named competitors, brokerages, or other coaches.
- No fabricated testimonials, names, or stats.
- Keep it actionable — every post should leave the agent with one thing they
  can DO, not just feel.

## Audience pains (mirror these in their own words)
"I'm working all day but deals aren't closing." · "My pipeline dries up every
few months." · "I'm terrible at follow-up." · "I don't know what to post."
"Leads ghost me." · "I'm busy but broke." · "I freeze on the listing
presentation." · "I keep discounting my commission."

## Audience desires
Consistent monthly income · a repeatable lead system · confidence on calls ·
more listings · a real schedule (not chaos) · respect as a pro · time back.

## Copy rules for THIS brand
- Open with a scroll-stopping hook (a bold claim, a pointed question, or a
  myth to bust) — the first line must earn the second.
- Be specific, not vague. "Send 5 texts before 9am" beats "follow up more."
- One idea per post, one clear next step.
- Plain talk, short sentences, like a top broker texting a friend. No jargon.
- Confidence without hype. No "get rich," no fake numbers.
