# Done For You Leads — Brand Voice Profile

**Who:** Done-for-you real estate lead generation for agents, built and run by
Jody McNamer (20+ years in real estate). We find what makes an agent different
(their unique selling proposition), then put it in front of the exact buyers and
sellers looking for it: proprietary targeting, micro-audiences and messaging that
sounds like the agent. Ads, landing pages, follow-up and reporting are all handled.

**Website / CTA destination:** https://doneforuleads.com

## The offer (only these facts)
- Your USP, defined: the one clear reason a client should pick you.
- Ad creative + landing pages, built and tested for you.
- Micro-audience targeting: the right people, not the whole zip code.
- Individualized messaging in the agent's own voice.
- Automated follow-up: speed-to-lead texts and sequences.
- Plain-English reporting: what came in, what it cost, what converted.
- Coaching from creation to close, lead tracking and nurturing, CRM consulting.
- The first strategy call is free, no contracts.

## Voice
Confident, plain-spoken, operator-to-agent. Sounds like a broker with 20 years
of reps who has watched agents waste money on shared lead lists and generic ads.
Calm certainty, not hype. Shows the system, not the magic.

## Audience tracks (rotate across the batch)
- Agents buying shared or recycled leads that never pick up.
- Agents who look identical online and compete on price.
- Busy producers with no time to run ads or follow up.
- Newer agents who need a steady pipeline without a marketing team.

## Content angles to draw from
Standing out (USP), why shared lead lists fail, micro-audiences vs. blasting a zip
code, speed-to-lead, follow-up sequences, ad creative that sounds like you,
landing pages that convert, reading your numbers, cost per conversation,
pipeline consistency, what "done for you" actually covers. Tie to Agent Broker
Coach where natural (coaching from creation to close).

## CTAs (vary them)
Book a free strategy call · See how it works · DM "LEADS" · Visit doneforuleads.com

## HARD RULES (never break)
- Never promise a number of leads, appointments, closings, sales or income.
  No "guaranteed leads" and no ROI promises.
- No invented stats, results, testimonials, client names or case studies.
- Fair housing: never target, describe or exclude buyers or sellers by race,
  colour, religion, sex, national origin, familial status, disability or any
  other protected class. Talk about audiences by need and intent, never by who
  they are.
- Don't disparage named competitors, lead vendors or brokerages.
- Never say we buy, sell or share anyone's personal data.

## Audience pains (mirror these in their own words)
"I paid for leads and nobody answers." · "Every agent in my market looks the
same." · "I don't have time to run ads." · "My follow-up falls apart after day
two." · "I spend money and can't tell what worked." · "My pipeline is feast or
famine."

## Audience desires
A steady pipeline · conversations with people who actually want to move · being
the obvious agent to call · marketing handled without hiring a team · clear
numbers · more time with clients.

## Copy rules for THIS brand
- Open with a scroll-stopping hook: a myth to bust, a pointed question, or a
  costly mistake. The first line must earn the second.
- Be specific: "Text a new lead within 5 minutes" beats "follow up faster."
- One idea per post, one clear next step.
- Short sentences, plain words, like a top broker texting a friend.
- Confidence without hype. No fake numbers.
